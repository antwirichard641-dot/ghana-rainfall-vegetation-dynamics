# Research Figures

This directory contains figures generated from the analysis of rainfall variability and satellite-derived vegetation dynamics in Ghana from 2000 to 2024.

## Figures

- `figure1_annual_rainfall_trend.png` — Annual rainfall variability and estimated linear trend.
- `figure2_annual_ndvi_trend.png` — Annual mean NDVI and estimated linear trend.
- `figure3_rainfall_ndvi_relationship.png` — Relationship between annual rainfall estimates and annual mean NDVI, including a fitted regression line.

All figures were generated in Python using the analysis dataset. NDVI represents vegetation greenness and is not a direct measurement of forage biomass or quality.
