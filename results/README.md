# Results

This directory contains the statistical outputs, summary tables, and figures produced by the research notebooks.

## Planned contents

- Annual rainfall variability summaries.
- Annual vegetation greenness (NDVI) summaries.
- Rainfall–NDVI correlation results.
- Detrended correlation results.
- Leave-one-year-out sensitivity analysis.
- Research figures and visualizations.

## Reproducibility

Results should be generated from the documented datasets and analysis notebooks. Statistical interpretations should distinguish association from causation and acknowledge the limitations of NDVI as a proxy for vegetation greenness rather than a direct measure of forage biomass or quality.
